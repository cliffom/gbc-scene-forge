Checks the current canvas against 8 background/window slots and 8 sprite slots. Includes hidden layers and all frames of animations linked to placed artwork.

Reserve slots for UI/window content, effects and dynamic actors. Leave blank if unknown; enter zero only when no additional slots are needed. Unknown reservations cannot pass preflight.
