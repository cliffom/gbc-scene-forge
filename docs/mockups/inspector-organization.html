01 · Identity without repetition

An unlabeled name above Width, Height and Total pixels. Frame-specific details and shared editing scope stay in Properties.

02 · Palette is a real section

Composition and Selected layer keep their distinct meaning. Swatches scroll inside the pane; choosing a color updates the drawing-color preview.

03 · Same structure, useful context

Selection rises when active. Validation and recovery remain reachable as quiet disclosures. Scene uses its own layers and selection controls.
